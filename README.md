# Leo

Reproducible experiments and reusable recipes for cost-effective AI orchestration, starting with NixOS engineering.

Leo studies when routing, cascades, and frontier-supervised open-weight workers reduce the total cost of independently verified work. Every claim should identify its task distribution, quality criteria, model and harness versions, accounting basis, and replication artifacts.

Named after Leonard Bernstein, Leo is a public orchestration project. Private workload evaluation and autonomous web research remain separate projects that can consume its recipes and evidence.

## Status

Project initialized on 2026-10-29. The first milestone is a small NixOS experiment with strong single-model baselines, independent acceptance tests, and complete cost accounting. No orchestration performance or savings have been demonstrated yet.

The initial scope is experiment design, accounting, and reusable recipes. Integrations and benchmark execution follow after the task fixtures and model identities are pinned.
